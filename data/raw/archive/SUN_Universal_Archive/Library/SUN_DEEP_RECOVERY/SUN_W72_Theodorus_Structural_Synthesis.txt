\begin{abstract}
We formalize a set of exact structural alignments among three independently specified objects: the SUN scale equation, the integer-radius subsequence of the Spiral of Theodorus, and the W72 finite-state/acoustic architecture. The SUN system is generated by two positive scale operators \(s_R=2/3\) and \(s_L=3/4\), whose composition is the half-scale transformation \(s_S=1/2\). The Theodorus radius law \(r_n=\sqrt n\) yields the distinguished subsequence \(r_1,r_4,r_9,r_{16}=1,2,3,4\), from which the same operators arise exactly and admit two inequivalent geometric histories for the transformation \(1/2\). Independently, W72 uses the reciprocal acoustic pair \(3/2\) and \(4/3\); in logarithmic octave coordinates these sum to \(+1\), whereas the SUN pair sums to \(-1\). Thus W72 is an exact multiplicative inverse of the SUN two-operator closure. We then analyze the W72 data model
\[
W_{72}=\mathbb Z_{12}\times\mathbb Z_3\times\mathbb Z_2,
\]
its thirty-six binary fibers, and the fiber-preserving \(72=70+2\) partition. The historical \(70/72\) material is treated only as secondary structural instantiation, not as causal evidence. Finally, we formulate a falsifiable geometric closure problem connecting the SUN/Theodorus operator set to a constrained nine-triangle incidence system. The result is a layered validation framework that separates theorem-level identities, model-level correspondences, and unresolved empirical or historical claims.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The present work addresses a recurring methodological problem in mathematically structured comparative research: when several constructions exhibit the same small set of ratios, symmetries, or cardinalities, one must distinguish exact algebraic equivalence from merely suggestive resemblance. The central objective is therefore not to accumulate numerical coincidences, but to identify which alignments are forced by independently specified mathematical structures, which are consequences of a shared data model, and which remain interpretive or historical hypotheses.

The primary object is the SUN scale system. Let \(C>0\) be a carrier magnitude and define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sun-core}
R=\frac23 C,\qquad
L=\frac34 C,\qquad
C'=\frac{RL}{C}.
\end{equation}
The induced scale transformation is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sun-half}
C'=\frac12 C.
\end{equation}
The elementary identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sun-factor}
\frac23\cdot\frac34=\frac12
\end{equation}
is not by itself evidentiary. The relevant question is whether independently defined constructions reproduce the ordered operator pair, its composite, and its associated structural behavior without being tuned to the SUN equation.

Two such constructions are considered. The first is the Spiral of Theodorus, whose radial sequence is \(r_n=\sqrt n\). The perfect-square indices produce exact integer radii and thereby an intrinsic realization of the ordered ratios \(2/3\), \(3/4\), and \(1/2\). More importantly, the same spiral contains two distinct paths realizing the half-scale transformation, one direct and one compositional.

The second construction is the W72 architecture, a product-state system with a separate acoustic layer. Its acoustic operators use the reciprocal ratios \(3/2\) and \(4/3\), whose product is \(2\). The resulting transformation is therefore the exact multiplicative inverse of SUN's half-scale closure. W72 also introduces a finite data model,
\[
W_{72}=\mathbb Z_{12}\times\mathbb Z_3\times\mathbb Z_2,
\]
together with an involutive binary coordinate and a canonical decomposition into thirty-six two-state fibers. This structure permits a precise statement of the \(72=70+2\) anomaly: removing one intact binary fiber leaves exactly thirty-five intact fibers, hence \(70=35\times2\).

The historical \(70/72\) sources motivating part of the W72 analysis are methodologically distinct from the mathematics. They are therefore treated as a secondary dataset and not as evidence that the underlying mathematics caused, encoded, or was known by the relevant historical authors.

The objectives of this paper are:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
    \item to formalize the SUN operator algebra in a scale group;
    \item to prove the exact Theodorus realization and dual-route identity;
    \item to prove the reciprocal SUN--W72 octave duality;
    \item to formalize the W72 binary-fiber data model and its \(70/72\) partition theorem;
    \item to separate direct mathematical validation from structural and historical corroboration; and
    \item to formulate the unresolved geometric closure problem as a falsifiable system rather than a retrospective fit.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Mathematical Framework and Proofs}

\subsection{Validation levels}

\begin{definition}[Validation classes]
Let \(\mathcal M\) be a target mathematical model.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A \emph{direct mathematical validation} is an exact identity, isomorphism, inverse relation, or theorem obtained from an independently specified construction and matching a nontrivial component of \(\mathcal M\).
    \item A \emph{structural corroboration} is a correspondence of organization, invariance, or state-transition form that is mathematically exact within each system but depends on an explicit adapter between systems.
    \item A \emph{historical instantiation} is a source-attested realization of a structure already specified mathematically. It does not imply authorial intent, common causation, or historical transmission.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

This hierarchy is used throughout. In particular, numerical equality alone is not promoted to direct validation unless the compared quantities arise naturally from independently fixed structures.

\subsection{The SUN scale group}

Let \(G=(\Rpos,\cdot)\). Every positive scale operator is an element of \(G\), acting on \(C\in\Rpos\) by multiplication.

\begin{definition}[SUN operators]
Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sun-ops}
s_R=\frac23,\qquad
s_L=\frac34,\qquad
s_S=s_Rs_L.
\end{equation}
The associated action on \(C\) is
\[
T_a(C)=aC,\qquad a\in G.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[SUN closure]\label{prop:sunclosure}
The composite SUN operator is
\[
s_S=\frac12.
\]
Moreover \(T_{s_R}\) and \(T_{s_L}\) commute.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Since multiplication in \(\Rpos\) is abelian,
\[
s_Rs_L=\frac23\frac34=\frac{6}{12}=\frac12
=s_Ls_R.
\]
Therefore
\[
T_{s_R}\circ T_{s_L}=T_{s_L}\circ T_{s_R}=T_{1/2}.
\]
\end{proof}

The original carrier equation \eqref{eq:sun-core} is equivalent to Proposition~\ref{prop:sunclosure}, because
\[
\frac{RL}{C}
=\frac{(\frac23C)(\frac34C)}{C}
=\frac12 C.
\]

\subsection{Logarithmic representation}

The logarithm
\[
\ell:G\rightarrow(\mathbb R,+),\qquad
\ell(x)=\log_2 x
\]
is a group isomorphism. Hence
\begin{align}
\ell(s_R)&=\log_2\frac23,\\
\ell(s_L)&=\log_2\frac34,\\
\ell(s_S)&=\ell(s_R)+\ell(s_L)=-1.
\end{align}

Thus the SUN composite is exactly one negative octave in base-two logarithmic coordinates. This observation will provide the natural comparison with W72.

\subsection{Theodorus realization}

Let the Spiral of Theodorus be represented by the radius sequence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:theodorus-radius}
r_n=\sqrt n,\qquad n\geq1.
\end{equation}
At the perfect-square indices \(n=k^2\), one has \(r_{k^2}=k\).

\begin{theorem}[Theodorus operator realization]\label{thm:theodorus}
The consecutive perfect-square radii
\[
r_1=1,\qquad r_4=2,\qquad r_9=3,\qquad r_{16}=4
\]
induce exactly the SUN operator pair and its composite:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:theodorus-sun}
\frac{r_4}{r_9}=\frac23,\qquad
\frac{r_9}{r_{16}}=\frac34,\qquad
\frac{r_4}{r_{16}}=\frac12.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Substitution of \eqref{eq:theodorus-radius} gives
\[
r_4=2,\qquad r_9=3,\qquad r_{16}=4.
\]
Hence
\[
\frac{r_4}{r_9}=\frac23,\qquad
\frac{r_9}{r_{16}}=\frac34,
\]
and multiplication yields
\[
\frac{r_4}{r_9}\frac{r_9}{r_{16}}
=\frac{r_4}{r_{16}}
=\frac24
=\frac12.
\]
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dual-route half-scale identity]\label{thm:dualroute}
Within the same Theodorus construction, the scale transformation \(1/2\) has two distinct geometric histories:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dualroute}
\frac{r_1}{r_4}
=
\frac{r_4}{r_9}\frac{r_9}{r_{16}}
=
\frac12.
\end{equation}
The first route uses the radius transition \(1\to2\); the second uses \(2\to3\to4\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Since \(r_1=1\) and \(r_4=2\),
\[
\frac{r_1}{r_4}=\frac12.
\]
By Theorem~\ref{thm:theodorus},
\[
\frac{r_4}{r_9}\frac{r_9}{r_{16}}
=\frac23\frac34
=\frac12.
\]
The sequences of intermediate radii are different, so the paths are not identical as geometric histories even though their realized multiplicative scale factor is the same.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Relational identity versus register]
Theorem~\ref{thm:dualroute} is stronger than the arithmetic identity \(1/2=(2/3)(3/4)\) in one limited sense: both sides occur as naturally selected radial relations inside a single pre-existing geometric construction. The transformation is locally identical but occurs at different absolute radial registers.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The \(60^\circ\) and common-center features}

At the transition to \(r_4=2\), the corresponding right triangle has side lengths
\[
1,\sqrt3,2.
\]
Therefore it is a \(30^\circ\)-\(60^\circ\)-\(90^\circ\) triangle.

\begin{proposition}[Exact angular feature]
The Theodorus construction contains an exact \(60^\circ\) angle at the \(1:\sqrt3:2\) triangle.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For a right triangle with hypotenuse \(2\), short leg \(1\), and other leg \(\sqrt3\),
\[
\sin\theta=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}
\]
for the angle opposite the \(\sqrt3\) side. Hence \(\theta=60^\circ\).
\end{proof}

All component triangles of the classical construction share one pole \(O\). We retain this as a structural feature, but not as a proof of any external geometric correspondence.

\subsection{W72 state space}

The W72 state model separates hierarchical resolution, acoustic generation, angular address, ternary refinement, binary refinement, and acoustic residual:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:w72-state}
S=(c,g,p,\tau,\beta,\rho).
\end{equation}
The finite hierarchical carrier is
\begin{align}
W_{12}&=\mathbb Z_{12},\\
W_{36}&=\mathbb Z_{12}\times\mathbb Z_3,\\
W_{72}&=\mathbb Z_{12}\times\mathbb Z_3\times\mathbb Z_2.
\end{align}
The two refinement stages are therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:12-36-72}
12\xrightarrow{\times3}36\xrightarrow{\times2}72.
\end{equation}

The local phase space satisfies
\[
\mathbb Z_3\times\mathbb Z_2\cong\mathbb Z_6.
\]
Define
\[
T(\tau,\beta)=(\tau+1,\beta),\qquad
B(\tau,\beta)=(\tau,\beta+1),
\]
where addition is taken modulo \(3\) and \(2\), respectively. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:TB}
T^3=I,\qquad B^2=I,\qquad TB=BT.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Binary-fiber decomposition}

\begin{definition}[Binary fiber]
For each \((p,\tau)\in\mathbb Z_{12}\times\mathbb Z_3\), define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fiber}
F_{p,\tau}
=
\{(p,\tau,0),(p,\tau,1)\}.
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Binary-fiber decomposition]\label{thm:fibers}
The state space \(W_{72}\) decomposes into exactly thirty-six pairwise disjoint binary fibers:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fiber-decomp}
W_{72}
=
\coprod_{p\in\mathbb Z_{12}}
\coprod_{\tau\in\mathbb Z_3}
F_{p,\tau}.
\end{equation}
Each fiber is closed under the involution \(B\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
There are \(12\cdot3=36\) possible parent coordinates \((p,\tau)\). For each parent coordinate there are exactly two values of \(\beta\), so each fiber contains exactly two states. Distinct parent coordinates give disjoint fibers. Hence
\[
|W_{72}|=36\cdot2=72.
\]
Since \(B\) flips only the binary coordinate,
\[
B(p,\tau,0)=(p,\tau,1),\qquad
B(p,\tau,1)=(p,\tau,0),
\]
and therefore \(B(F_{p,\tau})=F_{p,\tau}\) and \(B^2=I\).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Fiber-preserving \(70+2\) partition]\label{thm:70+2}
If one complete binary fiber \(F_*\) is isolated from \(W_{72}\), then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:partition}
W_{72}=F_*\sqcup M_{70},
\end{equation}
where
\[
|F_*|=2,\qquad |M_{70}|=70,
\]
and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:70fibers}
M_{70}=35\times2
\end{equation}
as a union of thirty-five intact binary fibers.
Conversely, any fiber-preserving \(70+2\) partition isolates exactly one fiber.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Theorem~\ref{thm:fibers}, \(W_{72}\) contains thirty-six disjoint fibers of cardinality \(2\). Removing one complete fiber leaves thirty-five such fibers, hence
\[
72-2=70=35\cdot2.
\]
Conversely, a fiber-preserving subset of cardinality \(70\) must contain an integer number of two-state fibers, necessarily \(35\). The complement therefore has cardinality \(2\) and is the remaining complete fiber.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Coordinate constraint]\label{cor:coords}
If the exceptional states are denoted \(E\) and \(M\), then every realization of Theorem~\ref{thm:70+2} satisfies
\[
p_E=p_M,\qquad
\tau_E=\tau_M,\qquad
\beta_E\neq\beta_M.
\]
\end{corollary}

\subsection{W72 acoustic operators}

The W72 acoustic layer uses the exact logarithmic increments
\begin{equation}\label{eq:w72-acoustic}
\alpha=\log_2\frac32,\qquad
\lambda=\log_2\frac43.
\end{equation}
Because
\[
\frac32\cdot\frac43=2,
\]
one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:w72-octave}
\alpha+\lambda=1.
\end{equation}

For a nominal twelve-position wheel, write
\begin{align}
\alpha&=\frac7{12}+\delta,\\
\lambda&=\frac5{12}-\delta.
\end{align}
The residual \(\delta\) cancels under complementary composition. Thus the acoustic pair is locally complementary and globally advances by one octave/generation.

\begin{theorem}[Exact reciprocal SUN--W72 duality]\label{thm:reciprocal}
The W72 acoustic operator pair is the componentwise multiplicative inverse of the SUN scale operator pair:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:componentinverse}
\left(\frac32,\frac43\right)
=
\left(\left(\frac23\right)^{-1},
      \left(\frac34\right)^{-1}\right).
\end{equation}
Consequently,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:duality}
\left(\frac32\right)\left(\frac43\right)=2
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\left(\frac23\right)\left(\frac34\right)=\frac12,
\end{equation}
and in base-two logarithmic coordinates,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:logduality}
\log_2\frac32+\log_2\frac43=+1,\qquad
\log_2\frac23+\log_2\frac34=-1.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Direct inversion gives
\[
\left(\frac23\right)^{-1}=\frac32,\qquad
\left(\frac34\right)^{-1}=\frac43.
\]
Multiplying the first pair gives \(2\); multiplying the second gives \(1/2\). Applying \(\log_2\) converts inversion to sign reversal, yielding \eqref{eq:logduality}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Opposite octave translation]
Under the logarithmic scale isomorphism \(\ell(x)=\log_2 x\), the W72 complementary acoustic pair translates by \(+1\), whereas the SUN pair translates by \(-1\).
\end{corollary}

This is the strongest direct cross-system alignment established in the present synthesis: it is an exact inverse relation, not a fitted approximation.

\subsection{Axis separation and checksums}

A central W72 modeling rule is the separation of hierarchical and acoustic axes. Acoustic operators act on \((g,p,\rho)\), while phase operators act on \((\tau,\beta)\). Refinement maps introduce coordinates without silently changing existing addresses.

For a path \(P\), define the checksum
\begin{equation}\label{eq:checksum}
C(P)
=
(\Delta g,\Delta p,\Delta\tau,\Delta\beta,\Delta\rho).
\end{equation}
A claimed closed loop fails if its endpoint differs from its start in an undeclared coordinate.

This is structurally relevant to SUN because it formalizes the distinction between a local relational transformation and an absolute register. The analogy is not an isomorphism of the complete data models; it is a shared invariance principle.

Three observations follow.

First, the Theodorus construction does not merely contain the values \(2/3\), \(3/4\), and \(1/2\) somewhere in an unconstrained continuum. They are generated by the consecutive integer radii selected automatically by the perfect-square indices \(4,9,16\). This gives a compact exact realization of the SUN operator chain.

Second, the equality
\[
\frac12
=
\frac{r_1}{r_4}
=
\frac{r_4}{r_9}\frac{r_9}{r_{16}}
\]
realizes a path-equivalence phenomenon: distinct internal trajectories induce the same external scale map. In categorical language, one may regard the two paths as distinct morphism factorizations whose image under the multiplicative scale functor is identical. No categorical machinery is required for the result, but this formulation clarifies the distinction between path identity and realized transformation identity.

Third, W72 supplies the exact inverse pair. If the SUN composition is a contraction by one octave, the W72 composition is an expansion by one octave:
\[
T_{\SUN}=T_{1/2},\qquad
T_{\W}=T_2,\qquad
T_{\W}=T_{\SUN}^{-1}.
\]
This is a substantially stronger alignment than sharing a cardinality or an approximate ratio.

\subsection{Structural correspondence: local identity and global register}

The Theodorus and W72 constructions each distinguish local recurrence from global state.

In Theodorus geometry, the multiplicative transformation \(1/2\) is realized at different absolute radii. In W72 acoustics, nominal angular closure under complementary operators may coexist with a nonzero generation displacement:
\[
C(RL)=(1,0,0,0,0).
\]
Thus the local angular address returns while the global generation changes.

Repeated same-direction traversal further illustrates the distinction. With
\[
\alpha=\log_2\frac32,
\]
twelve rightward steps give
\[
12\alpha
=
7+\kappa,
\qquad
\kappa
=
\log_2\frac{(3/2)^{12}}{2^7},
\]
so a nominal twelve-position return does not imply an exact return in the full acoustic state. This is compatible with the general principle:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:relational-register}
\text{same local relational identity}
\;\not\Rightarrow\;
\text{same global register}.
\end{equation}

Equation~\eqref{eq:relational-register} is a structural corroboration rather than a theorem identifying the SUN and W72 state spaces. It is nevertheless useful because it prevents a common modeling error: collapsing an equivalence of transformations into an equality of absolute states.

\subsection{Binary-fiber interpretation of the \(70/72\) anomaly}

The W72 model resolves a \(70/72\) count difference internally by changing the number of complete binary fibers:
\[
70=35F,\qquad
72=36F,\qquad
F\cong\mathbb Z_2.
\]
Hence
\[
\Delta(72,70)=F.
\]
The invariant is not the raw cardinality but the two-state fiber grammar.

The associated historical corpus, as represented in the working source material, reports several structures that instantiate components of this model after the mathematical constraints are frozen: a \(12\times6=72\) initialization with a \(70+2\) reduction; a genealogically common parent assignment for the exceptional pair; a differentiated two-state role within that parent assignment; and later \(70\) or \(72\) deployments ``two by two.'' A separate reception tradition is reported to realize \(72=36\times2\).

These observations are methodologically downstream of Theorem~\ref{thm:70+2}. They do not establish that ancient or late-antique authors encoded \(W_{72}\), nor that the mathematical architecture caused the textual traditions. The appropriate conclusion is narrower: the source corpus contains reported structural realizations compatible with the already-specified fiber model.

\subsection{Relation to the Completed-Harmony convergence motif}

The thread-level Completed-Harmony abstraction reduces to two operators
\[
a=\frac23,\qquad b=\frac34,
\]
with
\[
ab=s=\frac12,
\]
together with the requirement that an internally inequivalent route realize the same terminal scale transformation.

Theorem~\ref{thm:dualroute} supplies an exact geometric carrier for that motif:
\[
\gamma_A:1\to2,\qquad
\gamma_B:2\to3\to4,
\]
with
\[
\Phi(\gamma_A)=\Phi(\gamma_B)=\frac12.
\]
Accordingly, the Completed-Harmony contribution is best interpreted as a convergence principle rather than as an additional numerical source. Its mathematically relevant content is path inequivalence with endpoint-equivalent realization.

\subsection{Open constrained geometry problem}

The Theodorus construction supplies four features relevant to the proposed nine-triangle incidence experiment:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:key}
\left(
O,\,
60^\circ,\,
\frac23,\,
\frac34,\,
\frac12
\right).
\end{equation}
Using an arbitrary carrier \(C\), define the four scale states
\begin{equation}\label{eq:four-scales}
U=
\left(
C,\,
\frac34C,\,
\frac23C,\,
\frac12C
\right).
\end{equation}

The unresolved problem is not to fit these values to a pre-existing drawing. Instead, the values in \eqref{eq:key}--\eqref{eq:four-scales} are frozen before solving the remaining incidence equations.

\begin{definition}[Nine-triangle closure problem]
Fix:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
    \item a common center \(O\);
    \item one exact \(60^\circ\) central angular condition;
    \item four prescribed upward scale states \(U\) from \eqref{eq:four-scales};
    \item apex-to-base incidence constraints;
    \item the required concurrency pattern; and
    \item the prescribed chamber and outer-boundary conditions of the target nine-triangle configuration.
\end{enumerate}
Determine whether five opposing triangles exist satisfying all constraints exactly.
\end{definition}

There are three mathematically distinct outcomes:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item no solution;
    \item a finite or continuous nonunique solution family; or
    \item a unique exact solution.
\end{enumerate}
Only the third would support a strong geometric closure claim. The first would falsify the proposed adapter, while the second would show that additional independent constraints are required.

This experiment remains open and is not included among the validated results of the present paper.

\subsection{Dependence and non-independence}

The evidentiary layers are not statistically independent. The SUN and Completed-Harmony formulations share the same operator values by construction. Theodorus geometry is independent of those formulations in the sense that its radius law is externally fixed, but once the perfect-square subsequence is selected, the derived ratios telescope algebraically. W72 is independently specified at the architectural level, but its acoustic pair is explicitly the classical reciprocal pair \(3/2\), \(4/3\). Historical sources within a single textual or reception lineage may also be literarily dependent.

Consequently, no probabilistic significance claim is made here. The appropriate mathematical result is exact structural compatibility and reciprocal equivalence, not a numerical estimate of chance coincidence.

\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

The synthesis establishes four principal results.

First, the SUN system is an abelian two-operator scale algebra with exact composite
\[
\frac23\cdot\frac34=\frac12.
\]
Second, the Spiral of Theodorus realizes the same ordered ratios at the naturally distinguished integer radii
\[
1,2,3,4
\]
and supplies two geometrically distinct histories for the same half-scale transformation. This gives an exact carrier for the distinction between internal path and external realized map.

Third, the W72 acoustic system is the exact multiplicative inverse of SUN:
\[
\left(\frac32,\frac43\right)
=
\left(\frac23,\frac34\right)^{-1}.
\]
The corresponding logarithmic translations are \(+1\) and \(-1\) octave, respectively. This reciprocal duality is the strongest independent algebraic validation identified in the present framework.

Fourth, the W72 finite-state model admits a canonical decomposition into thirty-six binary fibers. Isolating one complete fiber yields
\[
72=2+70=1(2)+35(2),
\]
and forces the exceptional pair to share the same \((p,\tau)\) coordinates while occupying opposite binary states. The historical \(70/72\) corpus may instantiate this grammar, but such compatibility is not equivalent to proof of historical encoding or causation.

The immediate future work is computational rather than interpretive. The nine-triangle closure problem should be encoded as an exact algebraic or interval-arithmetic constraint system with the SUN/Theodorus parameters frozen in advance. A successful result must report solution multiplicity, algebraic residuals, numerical conditioning, and sensitivity to each constraint. Separately, the W72 historical layer should be subjected to source-critical auditing that distinguishes independent witnesses from dependent reception traditions.
