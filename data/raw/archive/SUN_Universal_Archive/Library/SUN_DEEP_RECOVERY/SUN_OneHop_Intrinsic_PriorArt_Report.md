# SUN — One-Hop Intrinsic Prior-Art Pass

## Scope rule
This pass is deliberately bounded to **one hop** from already-admitted SUN/music mathematics, with a **hard cap of 10 attachments**.

An item qualifies only if it:
1. directly defines an admitted anchor;
2. is canonically generated by that anchor; or
3. is a canonical equation/function that acts on that anchor's native mathematical object.

No second-hop expansion was permitted.

## Ten intrinsic attachments

1. **Theodorus radial recurrence**
   `r_n=sqrt(n)` and `r_(n+1)^2=r_n^2+1`.

2. **Theodorus angular recurrence**
   `alpha_n=arctan(1/sqrt(n))`, `phi_(n+1)=phi_n+alpha_n`, with polar/complex realization `z_n=sqrt(n)e^(i phi_n)`.

3. **Pythagorean comma from the fifth/octave generators**
   `(3/2)^12 / 2^7 = 531441/524288`.

4. **Syntonic comma / Ptolemaic tetrachord family**
   `81/80=(9/8)/(10/9)`, `(9/8)/(81/80)=10/9`, `(256/243)(81/80)=16/15`,
   and `(16/15)(9/8)(10/9)=4/3`.

5. **Log-frequency / equal-temperament inverse pair**
   `c=1200 log2(f2/f1)`, `f_n=f_0 2^(n/12)`, `n=12 log2(f_n/f_0)`.

6. **Lewin GIS composition**
   `int(r,s) int(s,t)=int(r,t)` plus the unique-action/simply-transitive condition.

7. **Z12 pitch-class action**
   `T_n(x)=x+n mod 12`, `I_n(x)=n-x mod 12`.

8. **Tymoczko chord orbifold**
   `S^1=R/12Z`, `T^n=(R/12Z)^n`, `O_n=T^n/S_n`.

9. **Voltage-graph lift**
   `alpha(e^-1)=alpha(e)^-1`, `V(X^alpha)=V(X)×Gamma`,
   and lifted edge `(u,g)->(v,g alpha(e))`.

10. **Somos-4 / Laurent quotient recurrence**
    `a_n a_(n-4)=a_(n-1)a_(n-3)+a_(n-2)^2`.

## Result
Every one of these equations lands inside a functional category already present in the frozen taxonomy. The pass therefore **does not reopen the mathematical taxonomy**.

The most useful new observation is the Theodorus angular formulation: the spiral itself contains a native separation between a **local angle increment** and a **globally accumulated angular address**. That gives us a legitimate structural comparator for SUN's local-relation/global-address distinction, but it remains a comparator rather than evidence of identity.

The one-hop prior-art phase is now complete.
