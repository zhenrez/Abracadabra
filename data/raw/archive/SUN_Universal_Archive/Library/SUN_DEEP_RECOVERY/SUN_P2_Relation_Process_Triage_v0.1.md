## High-value families for proof next
- **TRANSFORMATIONAL_RELATION** — exact actions/compositions already closest to a generator algebra.
- **COMPLEMENTARY_INTERLOCK** — likely source of genuinely higher-order/n-ary hyperedges.
- **DIRECTED_COURSE_OR_MODAL_TRANSITION** — path/history-sensitive state transitions.
- **CYCLIC_TEMPORAL_ORGANIZATION** — recurrence, phase, cycle and address laws.
- **HIERARCHICAL_REDUCTION_OR_STRUCTURE** — potential reduction/generative grammars.
- **TRAJECTORY_OR_MORPHOLOGICAL_TRANSFORM** — path-bearing transformations.
- **ROTATION_OR_MODE_DERIVATION** and **SYMMETRY_LIMITED_ORBIT** — finite orbit/generator structures.
- **STOCHASTIC_TRANSITION_LAW** — stochastic rather than deterministic generator semantics.

## Important negative reduction
Several P2 records are mappings, measures, carriers, perception processes, or realization relations. Those remain first-class hypergraph objects but should not inflate the generator alphabet.
