## Evidence vocabulary
- **T** — attested in the consulted textual witness, not necessarily historical fact in modern terms.
- **M** — mathematical identity demonstrable under specified definitions.
- **S** — structural analogy that preserves stated invariants but does not establish historical equivalence.
- **H** — hypothesis not independently verified; cannot be locked.
- **C** — contradicted by sourced facts or by the construction's definitions.
- **P** — provenance needs recovery, independent full-text comparison, or a named version.

## Genealogy contradiction tests
1. In Irenaeus I.1: parent(Ogdoad) ≠ parent(Decad) ≠ parent(Duodecad). The immediate producers of Decad and Duodecad are distinct earlier couples; any attempted sequential class genealogy requires separate operational notion of 'class follows class' versus literal parentage.
2. Sophia 22nd is in *Decad+Duodecad concatenation*, but 30th of total aeons including Ogdoad. The same entity may have multiple addresses only with labeled coordinate systems.
3. Sethian Yaldabaoth is born from Sophia; Valentinian Enthymesis material generates a different downstream account. Mark cross-system comparisons, not a single quoted genealogy.
4. Hesiod's Titan order is Oceanus, Coeus, Crius, Hyperion, Iapetus, Theia, Rhea, Themis, Mnemosyne, Phoebe, Tethys, Cronus. Earlier user archive contains an assistant-created pair-order arrangement not equivalent to Hesiod's birth order.
5. Sabaoth and the principal Yaldabaoth are not simply Zeus and Saturn with interchangeable note addresses; that is a comparative identification requiring independent source qualification.
6. No demonstrated source makes one note both Jesus and Lucifer, although morning-star language supports a shared sign/function in particular contexts.
7. Moon phases, lunar nodes Rahu/Ketu, synodic Venus phases, Indian lunar mansions and solar zodiac are distinct astronomical coordinate systems and must not be collapsed.
