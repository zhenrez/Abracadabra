# SUN Protocol Amendment — P0 PRIOR-ART COMPRESSION
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-09-30
Status: MANDATORY FRONT-END GATE

## Governing workflow

PRIOR ART
→ RESEARCH-SPACE REDUCTION
→ SUN DERIVATION
→ PREDICTION
→ TEST

No substantial SUN-specific derivation begins until P0 is complete.

## Purpose

P0 is a work-elimination engine. It must identify:
1. established theory that already solves the proposed mechanism;
2. mathematical formulations equivalent to proposed SUN language;
3. lawful submechanisms that can be imported;
4. constraints that delete impossible routes;
5. counterexamples that prevent universal claims;
6. bridge mechanisms between layers/carriers;
7. open problems where established literature stops.

## Operational statuses

Every finding receives exactly one:
- SOLVES
- SUPPLIES_COMPONENT
- CONSTRAINS
- EQUIVALENT_FORMULATION
- BRIDGE_CANDIDATE
- COUNTEREXAMPLE
- ORTHOGONAL
- OPEN_PROBLEM

## Breadth-before-depth rule

Before a narrow mechanism dive:
- survey broad reviews, standards, field overviews and competing mechanisms;
- record search neighborhoods and representative sources;
- identify duplicate/equivalent mechanisms;
- only then narrow onto unresolved SUN-specific work.

## Required output

Every P0 pass produces:
- SOURCE_INVENTORY
- MECHANISM_MAP
- WORK_ELIMINATION_LEDGER
- CONSTRAINT_LEDGER
- OPEN_PROBLEM_LEDGER
- REVISED_RESEARCH_TREE
- GO/NO-GO decision for SUN derivation

## Non-circularity

Prior art may eliminate work, supply mathematics, constrain hypotheses,
identify adapters, and define nulls.

Prior art may not be retrofitted as evidence that SUN predicted something it did not,
alter frozen SUN predictions after testing begins, or erase residuals/counterexamples.
