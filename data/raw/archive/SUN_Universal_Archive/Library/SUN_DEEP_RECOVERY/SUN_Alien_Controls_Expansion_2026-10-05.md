## Exact witnesses and bounded conclusions

The following deductions test the grammar directly. They are not SUN derivations.

### 1. A genuine all-representations failure

Let \(S\) be the set of distinctions required by a declared contract, with \(|S|=N\). Let a fixed encoding budget allow exactly \(b\) binary digits per item, with no uncounted side information or variable message length:

\[
\mathcal R_b=\{q:S\to\{0,1\}^b\},\qquad
\mathcal C_b\supseteq\{q\text{ is injective}\}.
\]

Then

\[
N>2^b\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\nexists q\in\mathcal R_b\text{ satisfying }\mathcal C_b.
\]

This is a pigeonhole theorem over the entire declared family, not a collision in one selected encoding. It is a positive control for the strengthened saturation definition.

For pure finite encoding, \(b_{\min}=\lceil\log_2N\rceil\), for \(N\ge1\). Within the extension family that changes only the bit budget, the minimum extra budget is \(\max(0,b_{\min}-b)\). This is a concrete conditional minimality result. It does not prove compatibility with additional operator, realization, composition, type, or provenance constraints.

The result forces a change only if those distinctions and constraints remain required. Longer messages, side information, another alphabet, or tolerated loss alter resources or requirements. None is automatically a geometric-dimension lift. This finite storage argument is distinct from Shannon's noisy-channel rate theorems.

### 2. A derived coordinate cannot repair an existing collision

If

\[
q'(x)=(q(x),f(q(x))),
\]

then, for every \(x,y\),

\[
q'(x)=q'(y)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad q(x)=q(y).
\]

Thus the equality classes of the encoding are unchanged. A derived field can improve computation, access, display, or error checking without supplying a new independent distinction. SQLite generated columns provide a concrete instance of such dependence.

The test applies only when the new field is a function of the **retained encoding**. A field \(z(x)\) computed from additional source information may separate \(q(x)=q(y)\); that additional information must be counted. Appending \(z\) is not automatically independent either: admissible dependencies and realization constraints still need checking.

For dial-stack proposals, examine whether angular displacement, radial scale, generation, and grade are independently selectable under the stated model, determined functions of existing coordinates, or records of independent history. Counting symbols does not answer this question. The theorem says nothing about algebraic rank or manifold dimension until the relevant structure is specified.

### 3. Memory can resolve a state description without changing its class at every step

A generic non-inverting hysteretic relay can be modeled by

\[
s_{t+1}=\begin{cases}
0,&u_t\le u_-,\\
1,&u_t\ge u_+,\\
s_t,&u_-<u_t<u_+,
\end{cases}
\qquad u_-<u_+.
\]

Inside the threshold band, the same input permits different outputs depending on retained state. A memoryless description fails; a declared stateful description succeeds. This normalized example is not the exact inverting transfer law of the cited TI device.

Repairing an analyst's incomplete description is not evidence that the physical system automatically generates a new state class. Nor must the retained summary preserve all provenance: that depends on the contract.

### 4. Merge need not possess an inverse or retain a unique history

On sets, define \(A\vee B=A\cup B\). Join is associative, commutative, and idempotent. For fixed nonempty \(B\), the map \(A\mapsto A\cup B\) is not injective: \(\varnothing\) and \(B\) both map to \(B\). Hence it has no inverse on that domain.

Distinct histories such as adding the same element once or repeatedly can reach equal content. A universal requirement to retain all history is stronger than this content contract. Adding a history log would stipulate a richer model, not derive it from set union. This control concerns state-based semilattice CRDTs; it does not impose this exact algebra on every CRDT design.

### 5. Observation insufficiency is not the finite-code theorem

For two possible hidden states with positive priors, if one observation has equal nonzero likelihood under both, Bayes' rule leaves their posterior odds equal to their prior odds. That observation does not identify the state exactly. Enlarging storage or renaming the observation cannot recover evidence that was not acquired.

This bounded example concerns one observation with equal likelihoods. Other observation sequences or interventions may distinguish the states. A general identifiability claim needs its own model and proof.

### 6. Dynamics and computation do not by themselves supply a lift trigger

Rule 110's infinite ambient configuration space and update rule are already specified. Its universality construction therefore supplies a control against inferring class enlargement merely from sophisticated computation. The amount of computational workspace used in a particular construction is a separate question.

Cook also identifies persistent signals in this particular construction that encode the simulated cyclic tag system's acceptance/rejection history. This strengthens the history candidate while separating **retaining a process trace in an available carrier** from **enlarging the ambient state class**. It does not establish that every Rule 110 trajectory preserves complete history, or that an additive SUN grade already supplies such a trace.

Likewise, in the selected fixed-domain reaction–diffusion abstraction, let

\[
\partial_tu=F(u)+D\Delta u.
\]

Near a homogeneous equilibrium with Jacobian \(J\), a Laplacian mode of eigenvalue \(-\mu\) evolves according to \(J-\mu D\). Positive real part of an eigenvalue gives linear instability of that mode. This modern schematic restatement is a candidate event condition, not a complete nonlinear pattern theorem. Pattern growth occurs within the already specified field space; it does not prove representational saturation or higher geometric dimension.

The fixed-domain restriction is deliberate. Neither control establishes that all biological development or all computational systems have unchanging resources or carriers.

## Consequences for the research sequence

The expanded grammar becomes more useful when it distinguishes **relation structures, state coordinates, operators, parameters, constraints, evidence, and diagnostic metadata**. Treating every discovered mechanism as another coordinate would reproduce the original ambiguity around “dimension.”

Two sharper pre-Divergence tests emerge:

1. **Independence/information test:** does a proposed object separate a required distinction, alter admissible operations, or change constraints? If it is merely a function of retained state, declare that dependence.
2. **Insufficiency-type test:** is the failure due to encoding capacity, observational access, controller memory, computational resources, incompatible operation laws, or an incorrect model? Only the first is immediately the finite-encoding saturation control; the others require their own formulation.

For any candidate preservation law, declare the nontrivial observable contract before selecting the adapter. An arbitrary constant projection can satisfy an equation such as \(\pi\tau=\pi\) while preserving none of the distinctions at issue. The recovered SUN projection has a stated base-state role; extending that law universally requires comparably explicit semantics.

The controls do **not** establish a universal sequence from mismatch to saturation to minimal enlargement. They show a bounded case where enlargement is necessary under fixed requirements, alongside cases where apparent growth is derived, where evidence is missing, where dynamics change within an existing space, and where histories are intentionally identified by a content model. A universal meta-lift hypothesis must accommodate these cases with independently declared scope, rather than redefining every counterexample as a hidden lift.

No change has been made to the P089/P113 inventory or to the current conclusions: grade/rank lifting and preservation with retained difference are recovered; an intrinsic capacity-growth law, saturation trigger, automatic geometric-dimension increase, and universal meta-lift law are not established by those witnesses.
