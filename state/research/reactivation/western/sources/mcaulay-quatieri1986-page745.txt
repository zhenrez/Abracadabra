111. ESTIMATION OF SPEECH PARAMETERS 
The problem in analysis/synthesis is to take a speech 
waveform, extract the parameters that represent a quasistationary portion of that waveform, and use those parameters or coded versions of them to reconstruct an approximation that is “as close as possible’’ to the original 
speech. Furthermore, it is desirable to have a robust parameter extraction algorithm since the speech signal in 
many cases is contaminated by additive acoustic noise. 
The general identification problem in which the speech 
signal is to be represented by multiple sine waves is a 
difficult one to solve analytically. Therefore, the approach 
taken here will be pragmatic, in the sense that an estimator will be derived based on a set of idealized assumptions; then, once the structure of the ideal estimator is 
known, modifications will be made as the assumptions are 
relaxed to better model practical speech waveforms.
