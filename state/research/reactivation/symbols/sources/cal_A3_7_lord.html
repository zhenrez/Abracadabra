mry, mryˀ (mārē, māryā, mārā) n.m. master, lord
1 master Com. ▶ more

--(a) human Com.

(a.1) vs. ˁbd "servant" as an epistolary title of respect OfA-Egypt.

--(b) divine Com.

(b.1) ܡܵܪܝܵܐ : the Lord, God Syr.

(b.2) ܡܵܪܝ : my Lord, usually in reference to Jesus Syr.
2 owner Com. ▶ more

--(a) (of a text) author Syr.
3 title of respect Com. ▶ more

--(a) ܡܵܪܝ : clerical title Syr.

--(b) w. first pers. suffix (מר٠ מור) in respectfully addressing a man : sir JBA.

(b.1) in contrast: one scholar, the other scholar JBA.

--(c) pl.: neighbors Syr.
4 in the construct state as the head of many compound expressions Com. ▶ more

--(a) chief of ... Com.

--(b) owner of ... Com.

(b.2) ܡܪܝ ܢܟܣ̈ܐ : patrician Syr.

--(c) master of (i.e. specialist in) ... Com.

--(d) an element of many PN's JBA.

--(e) mariba : "sir father" = "respected senior", but used euphemistically Man.
