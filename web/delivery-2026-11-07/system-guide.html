How to read this guide and its limits

Each view retains the identities, units and links in its source. A unit is a kind of record; it is not automatically a generation. Unassigned generation and class values remain unassigned. Tree depth, display order and musical position do not supply a missing generation.

Upstream and downstream counts include the named relationship types in this view. They are not automatically family trees. Production, membership, command and proposed correspondence remain different relations. Aliases name the same record; a comparison between records does not merge them.

Anonymous membership slots are counted as occurrences, separately from other record IDs. They do not establish unique people or unique legions across sources. Relations outside the selected source view are not silently imported.

GEN: supplied generation. MEM: recorded membership and extent. TIME: supplied timing or relative duration. ADR: source address and position. FUNC: explicit role. TRN: recorded derivation or transformation. Unknown fields are shown instead of filled from an analogy.
