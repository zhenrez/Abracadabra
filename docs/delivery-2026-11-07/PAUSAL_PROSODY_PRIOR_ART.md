# Pausal forms: reusable Tiberian prosodic prior art

**Written accent alone does not determine a pausal form.** DeCaen and Dresher supply an existing account with distinct phonological and intonational domains, native transformations and concrete counterexamples. This is useful prior art for a context-sensitive operation register; it does not establish those Tiberian conditions in a Babylonian bowl.

Primary source: Vincent DeCaen and B. Elan Dresher2020, “Pausal Forms and Prosodic Structure in Tiberian Hebrew,” starting printed331, in *Studies in Semitic Vocalisation and Reading Traditions*. The already acquired [primary volume](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/gdc/gdcebookspublic/20/19/39/45/70/2019394570/2019394570.pdf) was inspected through its Exa text rendering, sections1–5. Source identity and exact spans are in `state/delivery-2026-11-07/pausal-operations/prosody/result.json`.

Prose and poetic accent systems have different transformation conditions. An operation must retain which system it belongs to. The proposed earlierI/P structure and transformation history are analyses, not direct recordings of natural intonation.

## Syntax, rhythm and morphology remain separate conditions

Syntax constrains candidate boundaries, but the authors also retain phrase length, tempo, rhetorical pause, emphasis, verse position and semantics. Pausal morphology can differ by vowel quality, preservation versus reduction of a vowel, or stress placement. The stress alternation even reverses direction across form classes. A blanket “move stress left” or “replacea withqameṣ” operation would lose these distinctions (P5–P6).

Apparent pausal tokens require lexical/morphological checking: plural forms can resemble singular pausal forms without participating in that alternation. The chapter retains fixed pausal idioms, possible scribal lapses and genuinely conflicting readings. Minor pause is explicitly outside its treatment. These exceptions prevent promotion of itsI-edge account to an exceptionless token classifier (P8).

Three short exact quotations are mechanically located in the captured rendering: “cannot be predicted from the accents.” (Q1), “not entirely determined” (Q2), and “virtual disjunctives” (Q3). ## P0-A fit and transfer boundary

Reuse the published native domains, alternant classes, division/simplification rules and adverse examples before inventing a new mapping. The prospective operator should require tradition, witness, lexical/morphological class, full verse hierarchy, clitic grouping, candidateI/P segmentation and exception status. This satisfies a bounded prior-art reuse unit; it does not finish the completeP0-A fourteen-question program or a token census.

For Molin's Babylonian bowl comparison, retain the proposed pausal and contextual forms as candidates. The bowl requires its own evidence for context and prosodic conditioning. A waw pattern alone does not demonstrateI, a Tiberian accent, a specific stress shift or the cause of a sound change. NoTiberian-to-Babylonian rule transfer, measured ancient duration/Hz or genealogical inference is licensed here.
