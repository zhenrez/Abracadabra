1. Scope and test discipline
Calibration corpus. The upper sequence is frozen from the Laozi and the early Han Huainanzi; the musical mechanism is 
frozen from the Lüshi Chunqiu and early Chinese pitch theory; wuxing and resonance are checked against modern specialist 
summaries. Later religious Daoist pantheons, Pangu mythology, and Neo-Confucian taijitu systems are excluded from this 
first test. The Huainanzi is especially suitable because its chapters 3–5 are the most comprehensive surviving statement of 
early Han cosmology, while chapter 6 develops resonance.[2][3]
Non-identity rule. A Chinese term is not declared to be a sefirah. The test asks whether an independently attested Chinese 
function can occupy the same musical address. Labels are costumes; preserved relations are the evidence.
Pass condition. The stack passes only if the bridge remains intact and the adapter preserves the independent order and paired 
or cyclic relations without inventing a missing Chinese entity. A function may be local, horizontal, or distributed; forcing all 
functions into a chronological vertical ladder counts as a failure.
Plain-language reading. The calibration is testing preserved function and relation, not identity of names. It passes only 
when the independent Chinese ordering and paired or cyclic relations survive without inventing a missing entity.

thousand things.[1] The identity of “Three” is not fixed by that line, so the test may not silently define it as Heaven–Earth–
Human.

11. Frozen prediction card for the later blind test
Before opening an unseen tradition, the adapter should predict the following functions, without predicting their names, number 
of gods, or narrative costume:
 an unmanifest or pre-determinate source outside the counted manifest hierarchy;
 a first carrier, reference, breath, word, light, tone, or continuum;
 paired expansive/generative and contractive/form-giving operations;
 a mediating relation whose interaction creates further differentiation;
 both reinforcing and limiting couplings;
 an emergent central or harmonizing collective mode;
 a recurrence mechanism and a distinct naming/measurement/address mechanism;
 a transmission or resonance interface linking separated levels;
 realized multiplicity; and
 an embodied human, ruler, ancestor, or microcosmic regulator that closes the feedback loop.
Blind-test rule. A prediction counts only if the target tradition independently supplies the function and preserves its relation to 
neighboring functions. Name similarity, later syncretism, and one-off poetic resemblance do not count.
Plain-language reading. The blind test predicts functions and neighboring relations only. It does not earn a hit from similar 
names, later syncretism, isolated poetic resemblance, a predicted number of gods, or a predicted narrative costume.
