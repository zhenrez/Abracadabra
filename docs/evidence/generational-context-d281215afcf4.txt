This is where SUN's existing distinction between nominal address and complete state becomes essential.

After twelve fifths, the pitch-class address returns nominally to zero, but the physical frequency ratio is not exactly unity:

\$$ P\_{12}=\kappa\neq1. \$$

Therefore, if the musical geometry requires exact closure, neither genealogy satisfies that requirement merely by reaching a twelve-step landmark.

## 3. A necessary correction to the proposed mirror test

The fourth-spiral relationship should be tested with the same care.

Two ascending fourths produce:

\$$ \left(\frac43\right)^2=\frac{16}{9}. \$$

That is correct. But \$16/9\$ is already within one octave, whereas two ascending fifths require octave reduction to produce \$9/8\$.

Consequently, the fifth and fourth adapters do not produce interchangeable trajectories merely because SUN's \$R\$ and \$L\$ compose to \$O\$. They describe different traversals, and any proposed mirror correspondence needs an explicit orientation transformation.

This matters because a genealogy that satisfies one traversal may fail the other.
