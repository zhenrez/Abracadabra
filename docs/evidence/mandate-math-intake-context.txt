## 1. The mode algebra is exact; its indices need to retain their meaning

These identities hold:

$$

$$

$$
h_mh_n=h_{m+n},
\qquad
h_m\overline{h_n}=h_{m-n},

$$

The Fibonacci representation also holds:

$$
b_0=0,\qquad b_1=1,\qquad
b_{n+2}=b_{n+1}+b_n,
$$

$$
h_{b_{n+2}}=h_{b_{n+1}}h_{b_n}.
$$

And your cumulative expression can be represented exactly:

$$
h_0h_1h_1h_2h_3h_5=h_{12}.
$$

**What has accumulated in that last expression is the harmonic-mode index.** It does not yet tell us that twelve chromatic positions, twelve entities, or twelve generational classes have been produced.

Once we specify

$$
\theta(t)=2\pi f_*t,
$$

the temporal frequency of $h_k$ is

$$
f_k=kf_*.
$$

Consequently,

$$
h_{12}\longleftrightarrow12f_*,
\qquad
h_{13}\longleftrightarrow13f_*.
$$

A chromatic coordinate uses a different evaluation. In twelve-tone equal temperament,

$$
f_j=f_*2^{j/12},
$$

so

$$
j=12\longleftrightarrow2f_*.
$$

The distinction is:

| Quantity | Mathematical meaning | What “12” evaluates to |
|---|---|---|
| Harmonic-mode index $k$ | Integer multiple of a reference frequency | $12f_*$ |
| Chromatic displacement $j$ | Number of semitone intervals | $2f_*$, in twelve-tone equal temperament |
| Cumulative Fibonacci value | Sum of specified sequence entries | $0+1+1+2+3+5=12$ |
| Population cardinality | Number of represented objects | Twelve objects, if an explicit counting rule gives that result |
| SUN register grade | Accumulated displacement under its specified evaluation | Governed by $2^{-g}$, independently of those counts |

The equal-tempered formula and the distinction between equal-tempered and exact pure intervals are standard musical facts. ([phys.unsw.edu.au](https://phys.unsw.edu.au/jw/tartini-temperament.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

These quantities can be related through a declared mapping. **Giving them the same numerical value does not establish that mapping.**
