# SUN / SOL - Cainan: The Five-Year Invariant and the Next Rhythmic Distinction

## 1\. Three witness traditions, not two

| Genealogical tradition | First begetting interval | Second interval | Difference |

The arithmetic exposes the transformation\:

$$
(35,30)\longrightarrow(135,130)
=(35+100,30+100).
$$

Consequently,

$$
\boxed{(a+100)-(b+100)=a-b=5.}
$$

This is an **additive invariant**, not an invariant under uniform tempo scaling.

The corresponding entrance ratios remain different\:

$$
\frac{35}{30}=\frac76,

\frac{135}{130}=\frac{27}{26}.

More importantly, the Samaritan genealogy demonstrates that **the expanded rhythm can exist without the expanded genealogy**\.

So the five-year difference cannot identify Cainan's presence, and the 27:26 entrance ratio cannot do so either.

This is a useful elimination: the timing evidence now tells us which quantities describe chronology and which actually distinguish genealogical membership.

## 4\. Why these equalities arise

We can identify their exact numerical causes without adding any operators.

Let $a$ and $b$ be the first two begetting intervals, $u_A$ and $u_B$ the respective years lived after those births, and $D_C$ the third person's total lifespan.

The existing event timing gives\:

$$
\boxed{
(d_1,d_2,d_3,d_4,d_5)
=
(a,\ b,\ u_A-b,\ b+u_B-u_A,\ D_C-u_B).
}
$$

In the Masoretic and Samaritan readings, $u_A=u_B$ $403/403 and 303/303 respectively), so \(d_4=b=d_2$\.

In the Greek reading, Cainan and Shelah share the same recorded 130-year begetting age and 330-year subsequent lifespan. This yields $D_C=460$, $u_B=330$, and therefore $d_5=130=d_2$\.

**The equal-duration reprise is a real mathematical consequence of each textual witness.** But because it derives from those same age records, it should not be counted as an additional independent piece of historical evidence.

The Clementine Latin reading demonstrates that an omitted Cainan does not necessarily entail the Masoretic temporal symmetry. The genealogical membership and the chronological transcription are separate constraints.
