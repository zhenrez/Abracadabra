## The distinction I missed

There are three different levels of validity:

1. Physical validity: Any two sounding notes produce interacting pressure waves. That alone establishes nothing about the intended musical structure.
2. Musical validity: A particular arrangement satisfies defined interval, harmonic, tuning, and voice-leading relationships.
3. Geometric validity: Those musical relationships realize the specific geometry and transformations predicted by SUN, rather than merely producing some mathematically describable shape.

A genealogical interpretation that preserves every person's identity and event order can still be musically wrong.

Conversely, if two competing textual reconstructions produce different musical structures, and only one satisfies the independently established harmonic and geometric constraints, that difference is potentially decisive evidence for choosing between them.
