## Symbol and sound packet: preserve transmission stages

A useful path is `glyph image -> grapheme in a witnessed script -> ordered alphabet position -> transliteration convention -> dated/dialectal pronunciation candidates -> word/morphology -> proposed musical or semantic relationship`. Numerical values, lexical meaning and acoustic properties are additional explicit maps, not aliases for the grapheme. Historical pronunciation alternatives can be correlated: preserve witness/stage constraints rather than taking every imagined sound combination as equally possible.

For one selected chain involving Paleo-Hebrew, Aramaic and English, preserve actual glyph forms and quoted words, not just normalized strings. Greek/Latin comparanda can test a transmission edge without imposing their alphabet on the others. Keep unknown vowels or sound values as alternatives with their dependency conditions. A decipherment result should predict something outside the exact words used to choose its key: another permitted transformation, a source location, an operational relationship or a withheld textual form.

Alphabet reversal is a well-defined operation only after a particular ordered alphabet is supplied. Its involution does not establish that the supplied alphabet order was historically intended, that a text uses that substitution, or that the symbols have particular sounds. Similarly, the numerical equality of two words can define a candidate association; it cannot by itself identify their meanings or phonetics. These are targeted inference boundaries, not a dismissal of the proposed investigation.

Ophiuchus remains open as an explicit or implicit relation: a symbol, phase/seam marker, operator, counted participant, or geometric region are different possibilities. Do not make “restoring orientation” secretly mean adding an equal thirteenth zodiac sector. A candidate must state which observables it changes and whether geometry changes at all.
