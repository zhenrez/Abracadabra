## Capture original grammar before proposing a mapping

| Check | Literal evidence to capture | Existing-model question |
| --- | --- | --- |

For every proposed correspondence retain three linked descriptions: what the original visibly states; the proposed mapping and its assumptions; and the existing candidate or relationship it constrains. A file whose contribution remains unresolved stays in the acquisition record.

## Evidence that can discriminate orientation

An independently grounded anchor plus an asymmetric ordered relationship can constrain an existing orientation: a named reference and directed successor, explicit handedness, an independently specified cardinal frame, or a documented class/register seam. Preserve the bridge assumptions connecting those source coordinates to the current model. Evaluate surviving candidates against the exact constraint and retain a reason for each survival or failure.

Symmetric shape, shared totals and matching labels can leave rotational or reflection symmetries unresolved. Record that remaining freedom. Do not turn a diagram's aesthetic alignment into a newly selected absolute orientation.
