## Executable interface

`validateHierarchyAtlas`, `filterHierarchy`, `hierarchyCensus`, `ancestorsOf`, and `reconcileLegionCatalogue` are exported by `src/hierarchy.ts`. Select multiple view IDs to combine independent origin and witness views; other explicitly supplied filters intersect that union. Cycles are reported by traversal without hanging. Count reconciliation rejects missing, multiply commanded, mis-sourced or wrongly indexed slots.

Validation covers shape, provenance references, declared dependencies and exact source-vector arithmetic. It does not adjudicate historical authenticity, infer cross-witness identity or choose a musical orientation. Actual Jev choices are preserved in the decision receipt; they are not mathematical proof.

## Explicit missing joins

- GAP-ABC-MONAD-JOIN: No source-independent identification of all primordial alternatives with one Monad or source-native cross-tradition birth chain.
- GAP-FULL-ARCHON-GRAPH: Complete named critical-edition archon/angel/demon parentage and appointment graphs remain to be recovered, not invented.
- GAP-NATIVE-ANGEL-ORDER: Imported72names retained separately; full native angelic hierarchy/order requires selected source witnesses.
- GAP-CHINESE-PANTHEONS: Early native sequence/operators are separate from later Chinese pantheons and the Western hierarchy.
- GAP-UNIQUE-LEGIONS: Commands/witnesses may overlap. Unique legion and personnel populations are unknown.
- GAP-MUSICAL-JOINS: Musical source trees may be merged as separate views; no numerical-total correspondence or invented parentage supplies their bridge.
